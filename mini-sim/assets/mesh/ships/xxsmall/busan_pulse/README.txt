Busan Pulse-inspired prototype. +X forward, Y up. Three colors; 3 static states: folded = wings folded, engines off; active = wings open, engines off; flight = wings open, blue static exhaust on both side and both lower thrusters. Wing tip details are lights, not engines. No animation, pilot, physics, textures or true glow; folding approximate. Not tested on Three.js r128 or Android.
